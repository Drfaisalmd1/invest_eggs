Monthly investment allocator

Each asset type has a risk score from 1 (very low) to 10 (very high). Enter your monthly amount and choose how much risk you're comfortable with, and the money is split so lower-risk assets get a bigger share. Change any score, switch an asset off, remove it, or add your own to see how the split moves.

This is a simple planning tool for education, not financial advice. The risk scores are general, simplified ratings that you can change, and they can't predict returns. Every investment can lose value, and you may get back less than you put in. Property and small businesses are hard to sell quickly, and monthly property money usually builds up towards a deposit or a property fund rather than buying property directly. Consider speaking to a regulated financial adviser before making decisions.
